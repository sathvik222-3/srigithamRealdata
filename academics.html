Academic Programs

Courses Offered

At Sri Githam, we offer the standard two-year intermediate education curriculum mandated by the Board of Intermediate Education, with integrated coaching for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, EAMCET, and CA Foundation.

MPC

Mathematics | Physics | Chemistry

The MPC stream is tailormade for students aspiring to pursue careers in Engineering, Architecture, and pure Sciences. Our integrated approach provides rigorous coaching for JEE (Mains & Advanced) and State EAMCET, alongside regular board exam preparation.

Engineering Focus
JEE/EAMCET Coaching
Advanced Laboratories
Daily Practice Tests

BiPC

Biology | Physics | Chemistry

Designed for future medical professionals, botanists, and zoologists. The BiPC program offers deep insights into life sciences with intensive NEET coaching. Our expert faculty ensures students are conceptually strong and competitively ready.

Medical/Dental Focus
NEET Coaching
Modern Bio Labs
Conceptual Learning

MEC

Mathematics | Economics | Commerce

The MEC stream perfectly bridges analytical mathematics with commerce and economics. It is the ideal stepping stone for careers in Chartered Accountancy (CA), Cost Accountancy (CMA), Business Management, and Finance.

Business & Finance Focus
CA Foundation Coaching
Commerce Lab
Market Analysis Skills

CEC

Commerce | Economics | Civics

CEC is designed for students aiming for careers in Civil Services (UPSC), Law, Business Administration, and Humanities. The curriculum builds a strong foundation in societal structures, economic principles, and accounting practices.

Civil Services Foundation
Law Entrance Prep (CLAT)
Debate & GD Sessions
Current Affairs Focus
