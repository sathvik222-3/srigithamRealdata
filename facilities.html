Hostel & Campus Life

A premier academic environment where students thrive, grow, and succeed.

Hostel Accommodation

Sri Githam Junior College provides safe, comfortable, and well-maintained hostel accommodations tailored to foster a focused study environment.

Boys Hostel

Our Boys Hostel offers a secure and conducive environment for focused academic preparation and personal growth.

  • Dedicated secure building blocks.
  • 24/7 supervision by experienced wardens.
  • Spacious, well-ventilated, and comfortably furnished rooms.
  • In-house medical assistance and regular health checkups.
  • Clean RO drinking water supply.
  • Dedicated study halls and recreation facilities (TV/Indoor Games).

Girls Hostel

Safety and comfort are our top priorities in the Girls Hostel, providing peace of mind for parents and students alike.

  • Highly secure, separate campus with stringent access control.
  • Round-the-clock female wardens and comprehensive CCTV security.
  • Comfortable, well-lit rooms with adequate storage.
  • Dedicated study environments free from distractions.
  • Hygienic washrooms with continuous water supply.
  • In-house medical assistance and regular health checkups.

Mess & Dining Facility

Nutritious Vegetarian Meals

A healthy body houses a healthy mind. Our expansive, highly hygienic dining hall is designed to cater to the dietary needs of growing teenagers, ensuring they have the energy required for arduous academic schedules.

  • 100% pure vegetarian & non-vegetarian, highly nutritious menu planned by dieticians.
  • Four robust meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner.
  • State-of-the-art automated kitchen ensuring absolute hygiene.
  • Large seating capacity to comfortably accommodate students.
  • Diverse menu that rotates regularly to prevent monotony.

Academic & Campus Amenities

Beyond accommodations, Sri Githam Junior College is equipped with cutting-edge academic infrastructure to facilitate premium learning experiences.

A/C Classrooms

Well-furnished, climate-controlled classrooms equipped with digital boards.

Modern Laboratories

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for hands-on practical learning.

Library & Reading Hall

Extensive collection of reference materials, journals, and a quiet reading hall.

Sports Facilities

Playgrounds and equipment for cricket, volleyball, badminton, and indoor games.

Day Scholar Transport Facility

For students preferring to attend classes and return home daily, we offer a highly reliable and safe transport network covering a wide radius.

  • Fleet of well-maintained, GPS-tracked buses.
  • Experienced drivers and dedicated attendants.
  • Seamless access to all campus amenities (library, labs) during working hours.
  • Highly secure campus entry/exit monitoring.
