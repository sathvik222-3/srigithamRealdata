Admission Guidelines

Admission Process

Our admission process is transparent and strictly based on merit.

  • Submit the online enquiry form or visit the campus physically.
  • Attend the basic counseling session with our academic coordinators.
  • Submit required documents for verification.
  • Payment of admission fees to confirm the seat.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates must have passed 10th Standard (SSC / CBSE / ICSE or equivalent).
  • A minimum aggregate of 60% for MPC/BiPC and 50% for MEC/CEC streams.

Required Documents

  • 10th Standard Original Marks Memo
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school
  • Study/Conduct Certificate
  • Community Certificate (if applicable)
  • 6 Passport size photographs
  • Aadhaar Card copy of the student

Important Dates

  • Online Registration: Opens March 1st, 2026
  • First Merit List: will be update
